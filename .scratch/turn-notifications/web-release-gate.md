For each surface, use this sequence and attach timestamped, redacted evidence or a specific failure for every item:

1. On a fresh browser profile/install or reset site permission and subscription, record the initial `Not enabled` state. Turn account consent on (no permission prompt should occur), explicitly select **Enable on this device**, grant permission, and record browser permission, subscription and server registration/`Ready` separately. Do **not** infer enrollment from permission alone. Repeat with permission denied (reset between cases): record `Blocked`, browser/OS recovery instructions, no automatic re-prompt and no `Ready`. If enrollment cannot be completed, record the failure and stop claiming this surface passes.
2. With the target room backgrounded or its tab/app closed (not just DevTools background simulation), have the other Player start a **new** Turn for the enrolled Player. Record accepted game transition and separate evidence for provider response, worker receipt/check, device notification display, and tap. A tap should focus an existing intended-room tab or open a new view, show the current room state and not replace another-room tab; on a signed-out/different account it should show the neutral original-Player sign-in gate. Note notification delay, missed delivery, OS grouping and anything not observable. Do not use DevTools' simulated Push button.
3. While the Player visibly **focuses that room** before the next Turn begins, start a different new Turn for them: there should be no new alert on any enrolled install for this Turn, even after focus is lost. Record whether no work was sent or whether a real delivered push reached the worker and was suppressed; these are different observations. An existing OS notification cannot be recalled.
4. Exercise a **stale push received after the Turn ends** with a real VAPID delivery (for example, controlled device disconnection and reconnection within the sender's short TTL, if the push service actually queues it). Record proof of delivery to the worker and that no notification displayed. If the provider never delivers that push, mark receipt-time stale suppression **not verified**, not passed. Do not bypass production verification or fabricate an event in DevTools to satisfy this step. Also check that a fresh eligible Turn **after** the suppressed push can still display.
5. Switch consent off, then cause another Turn: confirm registrations are removed and no alert arrives. Switch consent on again; confirm no silent re-enrollment, explicitly enroll again, sign out, then cause another Turn: confirm no alert or old-account room exposure. Capture whether a push was not sent versus delivered and suppressed. Sign back in, explicitly re-enroll if needed, and verify a **later eligible Turn** produces a fresh real notification/tap. Do not treat already displayed OS alerts as recallable.

## Decision rule and follow-ups

Pass the web gate only when **all three** required rows have observed actual VAPID delivery, display and tap plus the suppression, revocation, follow-up Turn and denial checks above, with honest limitations and no unresolved failure presented as `Ready` support. Log any failed or untestable case in a new `.scratch/turn-notifications/issues/` follow-up with surface, versions, deployment revision, expected/actual, redacted evidence and reproduction; link it here before changing the decision. A failed surface remains **NO-GO** for a general web-support claim until addressed and retested. Missing evidence is **unverified**, not a product defect and not a pass. Do not loosen receipt verification or claim exactly-once/guaranteed OS delivery based on a successful run.

Physical Android **TWA** sideload/Play-installed testing is tracked separately in [the Android device-check issue](issues/19-android-build-device-checks.md) and is **not** a web-release blocker. macOS Safari and iOS Home Screen apps have separate gates; a web pass does not unlock either.
