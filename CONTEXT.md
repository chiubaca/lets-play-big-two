# Big Two Rooms

The language of multiplayer Big Two tables and the people who join them.

## Language

**Spectator**:
A signed-in room visitor who watches live without occupying a player seat or taking turns. Spectators see played cards and remaining card counts, never unplayed hands; entering a room does not automatically make someone a player.
_Avoid_: Observer, viewer

**Player**:
A room participant occupying a seat who may receive a hand and take turns.
_Avoid_: Spectator

**Host**:
The first seated Player in a room, who can start and reset games. When that Player leaves the table, the next seated Player becomes Host.
_Avoid_: Room creator

**Room chat**:
Conversation within an online Big Two room, including messages from signed-in Players and Spectators. Its complete history belongs to the room for as long as that room exists and is available to any signed-in visitor, including someone who arrives after the conversation began.
_Avoid_: Table talk

**Inactive room**:
An online room with no connected signed-in visitors, whether or not any Player still occupies a seat or anyone has visited yet. A connected Spectator keeps a room active even if they do not play or chat; an inactive room expires after 48 hours, or after seven days if its game is unfinished.

**Chat message**:
A contribution to Room chat attributed to the sender's account name and their Player or Spectator role at the time it was sent; later name or role changes do not rewrite it. If the sender deletes their account, their name and text are redacted while the message's place in the conversation remains.

**Leave table**:
A Player gives up their seat in an online room and remains there as a Spectator. Their departure ends the current game for everyone at the table.

**Lobby**:
The place where people create, find, and join online rooms.

**Return to lobby**:
Going to the Lobby without giving up a seat in the room or ending its game.
_Avoid_: Leave table
