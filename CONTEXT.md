# Big Two Rooms

The language of multiplayer Big Two tables and the people who join them.

## Language

**Spectator**:
A signed-in room visitor who watches live without occupying a player seat or taking turns. Spectators see played cards and remaining card counts, never unplayed hands; entering a room does not automatically make someone a player.
_Avoid_: Observer, viewer

**Player**:
A room participant occupying a seat who may receive a hand and take turns.
_Avoid_: Spectator
